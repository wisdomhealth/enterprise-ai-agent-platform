# Administrator training and handoff session

Train the customer-designated platform, security, data, AI, support and privacy
owners in a runnable customer-controlled environment. Use sanitized fixtures; do
not demonstrate with production customer bodies or credentials.

## Required demonstrations

1. Staff OIDC admission, resource grants, administrator actions and audit lookup.
2. Drive/Gmail connector authorization, least-privilege scopes, revocation and
   explicit reauthorization.
3. Public chat credential lifecycle, customer/staff citation split, handoff queue,
   explicit Resume AI and resolution.
4. Email draft versioning, reviewer approval invalidation, uncertain-delivery
   reconciliation and safe retry boundaries.
5. Health/readiness versus liveness, dashboards, alerts, safe log fields and the
   [incident response procedure](../operations/incident-response.md).
6. Backup, guarded PITR, Redis-loss recovery and erasure-ledger replay using the
   [backup recovery runbook](../runbooks/backup-recovery.md).
7. Release evidence, known limits, scope/change control and the acceptance record.
